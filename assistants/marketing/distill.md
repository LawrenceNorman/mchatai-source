# What is durable for the Marketing Assistant

Keep, per piece: the slug, the title that shipped, the date, which channels it went to
and when, the readers' scores, the reads at seven days, and the one thing readers wanted
more of. Keep every taste note Lawrence states about public writing, quoted. Keep which
backlog topics are done and which were rejected, with the reason.

Drop: draft text, title candidates that lost, the lint output, capture commands, anything
about how a particular screenshot was taken. Those are reproducible from the playbook.
