# Music

## The sound identity

Before choosing a single instrument, read
`frameworks/loopstar/LOOPSTAR_SOUL.md`. It is the baseline, not a suggestion:
808s, synth voices, lush pads; never General-MIDI brass, winds, strings or
novelty patches. Tempo-synced delay, always.

## How to work here

**"Corny" is almost always the arrangement, not the instruments.** Before
swapping sounds, look at what is playing at once, where the space is, and whether
anything is answering anything.

Set key and BPM explicitly at the start of a session rather than inheriting
whatever was last used — an inherited key is the most common reason a part sounds
wrong for no visible reason.

**Verify by ear, not by meter.** Diagnostic meters can read a level for audio
that is not actually rendering. If you cannot confirm a change was heard, say so
rather than reporting success.

## What to learn

The user's instruments, range, tunings and what they are working towards. Ask;
none of it is recorded here.
