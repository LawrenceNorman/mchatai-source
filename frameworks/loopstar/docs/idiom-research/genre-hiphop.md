# Hip-Hop Family Idiom Source-Book — Boom-Bap · Trap · G-Funk · Grime · Lo-fi

**Conventions.** 4/4; beat positions 0..4 at 16th resolution (0.25 steps); triplets written .33/.67. Swing% = placement of the off-16th in its pair (50 = straight, 66.7 = full triplet). Velocity 0..127. Hook cells = (beat, dur) or (beat, dur, degree). Scale degrees are key-relative minor unless stated (1 2 b3 4 5 b6 b7; ' = octave up). Bass intervals are chord-relative (R, 3, 5, b7, oct, chrom = chromatic approach). GM program numbers are **0-indexed** (GM16 = Drawbar Organ convention). PD status: US, 2026 — compositions **published pre-1931** are public domain; famous recorded *arrangements* usually are NOT. All named patterns/hook examples below are original idiom constructions, not transcriptions.

## 1. 90s BOOM-BAP — 84-96 BPM, swung 16ths 54-62%, per-lane micro-timing

### 1.1 Canon
- Eric B. & Rakim — Paid in Full (1987): 2-hits-per-bar fingered-bass economy (interpolates Dennis Edwards "Don't Look Any Further"); drums from a chopped break.
- Pete Rock & CL Smooth — They Reminisce Over You (1992): major-key sax long-note hook over a 2-bar loop; THE horn-hook archetype.
- Nas — N.Y. State of Mind (1994, DJ Premier): dark 2-chord piano stab loop; riff-over-static-i blueprint.
- Nas — The World Is Yours (1994, Pete Rock): jazz piano loop + filtered upright bass, hook = piano fragment.
- Gang Starr — Mass Appeal (1994, Premier): 2-pitch detuned keyboard chop is the entire hook — proof hooks can be 2 notes.
- Gang Starr — Moment of Truth (1998, Premier): solemn bar-length piano pad chop.
- Wu-Tang Clan — C.R.E.A.M. (1993, RZA): 4-bar minor piano loop; the loop IS the chorus hook.
- Mobb Deep — Shook Ones Pt. II (1995, Havoc): detuned stab + semitone menace; 2-hit anchor bass.
- A Tribe Called Quest — Electric Relaxation (1993): lifted 4-chord min9 jazz cycle ("Mystic Brew") — the genre's harmonic ceiling.
- Souls of Mischief — 93 'til Infinity (1993): airy Rhodes comp + high answer figure in bar-tails.
- Jeru the Damaja — Come Clean (1994, Premier): pitched-percussion "water drop" as hook; counter-stabs with the snare.
- O.C. — Time's Up (1994, Buckwild): bass-led loop; horns as 1-bar answers every 4 bars.
- The Notorious B.I.G. — Unbelievable (1994, Premier): scratch-chorus arrangement archetype (cut vocal sample = hook).
- GZA — Liquid Swords (1995, RZA): eerie keys, chops placed off-grid deliberately.
- Slum Village — Fall in Love (2000, J Dilla): drunk swing, hazy Rhodes, ghost-note bass; snare lands late.
- J Dilla — So Far to Go (2006): per-lane micro-offsets (rushed kick vs dragged hat) as the groove itself.

### 1.2 Harmony
- Families: i7 one-chord vamp; i7–bVII (2 bars each); i7–iv7 dorian pair (im7–iv9); i–bVI–bVII; ii9–V13 vamp that never resolves; lifted 4-chord dorian jazz cycles (Electric Relaxation type, 1 chord/bar).
- Modal split ≈ dorian 50% / aeolian 35% / major-loop mixolydian-tinged 15% (T.R.O.Y. type I–IV feel).
- Extensions standard because sources are jazz/soul: min9, maj7, 13 voicings — plain triads read as fake. Menace color = bII stab or b5 in the loop.
- No cadences; loops never resolve. Harmonic rhythm: 1 chord per 1-2 bars; 2- or 4-bar loop; firmly riff-over-static.
### 1.3 Comping (chord lane)
- **BB-C1 "Premo one-stab"**: (0, 0.5, v110) + optional answer (2.5, 0.5, v96); staccato close-voiced minor add9 stab, register C3-C4. Ex: Mass Appeal, N.Y. State of Mind.
- **BB-C2 "Counter-stab w/ snare"**: (1.0, 0.25, v90), (3.0, 0.25, v90); rootless shell (3-b7). Ex: Come Clean.
- **BB-C3 "Bar pad chop"**: (0, 4.0, v85), new chord each bar, low-passed. Ex: C.R.E.A.M., Moment of Truth.
- **BB-C4 "Jazz-loop 4-hit"**: (0,1.0,v95)(1.5,0.5,v88)(2.0,1.0,v92)(3.5,0.5,v86), swing 57%; min9 rootless A/B voicings. Ex: Electric Relaxation.
- **BB-C5 "Dilla haze"**: (0,1.5,v78)(1.75,0.75,v68)(2.5,1.5,v84); ±20ms jitter per hit, vel range 65-90, Rhodes. Ex: Fall in Love.
### 1.4 Bass
- **BB-B1 "Fingered loop walk"**: (0,0.75,R)(1.5,0.5,b7)(2.0,0.75,R)(3.25,0.5,5); E1-A2; vel 90-105. Ex: Paid in Full lineage.
- **BB-B2 "Two-hit anchor"**: (0,1.5,R)(2.5,1.25,R or b7); dark, minimal. Ex: Shook Ones Pt. II.
- **BB-B3 "Dilla rubber"**: (0,0.5,R)(0.75,0.25,R ghost v45)(2.5,0.5,5)(3.5,0.5,chrom→next R); +15ms late overall. Ex: Fall in Love.
- **BB-B4 "Upright 2-feel"**: (0,1.9,R)(2.0,1.9,5); GM32; behind-beat. Ex: The World Is Yours.
- Dead notes (vel<50, dur 0.1) on 0.75/2.75 sell "played" bass. Never busier than 5 hits/bar.
### 1.5 Melodic hook grammar
- Scales: minor pentatonic + blue b5; dorian 6 as passing tone. Hooks are chopped-sample-like FRAGMENTS: 2-4 pitches, front-loaded bars, ≥40% of the bar silent.
- Cells: c1 [(0,0.5),(0.75,0.25),(1.5,0.5)]; c2 [(0,0.75),(2.5,0.5),(3.25,0.25)]; c3 [(1.0,0.5),(1.75,0.25),(2.5,1.0)] (skips downbeat); c4 [(0,0.25),(0.5,0.25),(1.0,1.0)]; c5 [(2.0,0.5),(2.75,0.25),(3.5,0.5)] (bar-tail answer); c6 [(0,1.5),(2.5,1.5)] (horn long-note); c7 [(0,0.5),(2.0,0.5)] (2-pitch Mass Appeal type).
- Contour: narrow (≤5th), oscillating around 1 or b3; ends on 1, b3, or 5 — never scalar runs. Repetition: same 1-bar cell x4, pitch varied only on bar 4 (turnaround); or 2-bar call+response. Register: C4-C6, 1-2 octaves above comp. Hook length 2 bars. Articulation: staccato (dur ≤0.5) reads as "chop"; swing matches drums.
- Original examples: **BB-H1** [(0,0.5,5),(0.75,0.25,b7),(1.5,0.5,1'),(2.5,0.5,b3'),(3.25,0.75,1')]. **BB-H2 horn**: [(0,1.5,b3'),(2.0,0.5,4'),(2.5,1.5,1')]. **BB-H3 2-pitch**: [(0,0.5,b3'),(2.5,0.5,1')] x3 then [(0,0.5,b3'),(2.0,0.5,4'),(3.0,0.5,1')].
### 1.6 Instrumentation (GM 0-indexed)
- Chords: GM4 Rhodes / GM0 piano (detune −8 cents) / GM11 vibes. Bass: GM32 upright or GM33 fingered. Hooks: GM56 trumpet, GM66 tenor sax, GM73 flute, GM11 vibes.
- Drums: break-feel — kick v100-115 doubled-up (0, 1.75, 2.5 typical), snare beats 1&3 v112-120 with rim layer, hats swung 16ths v55-85 alternating; low-pass ~8kHz vibe; no crash cymbals.
- Pack mapping: bassStyle=walking or rootDriven; swing 0.56-0.60.
### 1.7 Arrangement
- 2- or 4-bar loop for the whole track. Intro 4-8 bars = loop filtered (LPF) or bass muted; verse 16; hook 8 (add scratch/hook fragment layer); bridge = drop drums 2 bars.
- Energy comes from mute/unmute of lanes, not new material. Bass drops out under the last 2 bars of a 16 ("Premier drop") 
### 1.8 Corny traps
- Straight 16ths → dead. Fix: swing 56-60% + snare +10..25ms late, hat vel alternation ≥20.
- Triads → fake. Fix: min9/maj7 voicings only, rootless above C3.
- Chord changes every beat → wrong genre. Fix: 1 chord per 1-2 bars, max 4 chords.
- Busy scalar melody → fake. Fix: ≤4 pitches, ≤5 onsets/bar, 40%+ rest.
- Uniform velocity → machine. Fix: vel range ≥25 within every lane, ghost notes vel 40-50.
- Clean bright patches → wrong. Fix: prefer Rhodes/vibes over bright piano; keep melody ≤C6.
### 1.9 PD lifts (pre-1931 jazz/blues canon — boom-bap's grandparent repertoire)
- W.C. Handy — St. Louis Blues (1914); Turner Layton — After You've Gone (1918); Sweet Georgia Brown (1925); Ain't Misbehavin' (1929); Ellington — Black and Tan Fantasy (1927); trad — Careless Love; trad — St. James Infirmary (1929 "Joe Primrose" credit is on an arrangement — use the trad "Gambler's Blues" strain); Scott Joplin — The Entertainer (1902) + Solace (1909).
- Trap to flag: lift from pre-1931 SHEET versions; famous later recordings (e.g., Armstrong arrangements) remain protected.

## 2. MODERN TRAP — 130-160 BPM grid, half-time feel (snare on beat 2 i.e. position 2? no — snare at position 2 of 0..4 = beat 3), hats 16th/32nd

*Grid note: program at 140-150 BPM; kick sparse/syncopated, single clap/snare at position 2.0 (half-time backbeat), hats carry 16ths.*
### 2.1 Canon
- T.I. — 24's (2003, DJ Toomp): proto-trap brass stabs + rolling hats.
- Young Jeezy — Trap or Die (2005, Shawty Redd): sinister synth-string loop over static i.
- Gucci Mane — Lemonade (2009, Bangladesh): quarter-note piano/bell toll hook archetype.
- Waka Flocka Flame — Hard in da Paint (2010, Lex Luger): choir/brass wall, drop arrangement, 32nd hat rolls.
- Rick Ross — B.M.F. (2010, Lex Luger): one-chord menace; orchestra stab on beat 0 comping.
- Chief Keef — Love Sosa (2012, Young Chop): drill-adjacent choir + bell 8th arps.
- Migos — Versace (2013, Zaytoven): bouncing high keys/flute; triplet-flow trigger.
- Migos — Bad and Boujee (2016, Metro Boomin): 3-note pluck hook, space as the hook.
- Future — Mask Off (2017, Metro Boomin): minor-pent flute loop over static i, ~150 BPM half-time — the melodic-loop archetype.
- 21 Savage — Bank Account (2017, 21/Metro): 6-note descending cell repeated verbatim throughout.
- Travis Scott — Goosebumps (2016): detuned pluck, b6 color tone, dark psychedelia.
- Travis Scott — Sicko Mode (2018): 3-way beat-switch arrangement extreme.
- Lil Uzi Vert — XO Tour Llif3 (2017, TM88): melodic-trap exception — 4-chord synth loop, progression-driven.
- Playboi Carti — Magnolia (2017, Pi'erre Bourne): video-game pluck hook in call-response with ad-libs.
- Pop Smoke — Dior (2019, 808Melo): UK-drill crossover; sliding 808 as counter-melody.
- Future — March Madness (2015, Tarentino): pad-choir wash + pitched vocal haze.
### 2.2 Harmony
- Families: static i (+b2 or b6 neighbor) ~40%; i–bVI (2 bars each); i–bVI–bVII; i–bVI–III–bVII; i–iv; Andalusian i–bVII–bVI–V (V from harmonic minor) for epic menace; drill: i–V(harm. minor) 2-bar alternation. Melodic trap: aeolian pop loops bVI–bVII–i–i or i–bIII–bVII–bVI.
- Natural minor default; harmonic minor (raised 7) in arps for menace; phrygian b2 as stab/neighbor. Major keys effectively banned.
- Harmony often IMPLIED: 808 root + monophonic bell line; pads sustain bare 5th or add b6. No maj7s; min triads and min(add9) ok.
- Harmonic rhythm: 1-2 bars per chord; 4/8-bar loop; predominantly riff-over-static (melodic-trap subfamily is progression-driven).
### 2.3 Comping
- **TR-C1 "Dark hold"**: (0, 8.0 across 2 bars, v70) choir/string pad, bare 5th + b6. Ex: Hard in da Paint, March Madness.
- **TR-C2 "Bell 8th arp"**: 8 hits (0..3.5 step 0.5, dur 0.45), degrees cycle 1-b3-5-b6-5-b3, vel 75-95 accent on 0/2.0. Ex: Love Sosa.
- **TR-C3 "Stab on 1"**: (0, 0.75, v100) strings/brass once per bar, octave+5th voicing. Ex: B.M.F.
- **TR-C4 "Zay bounce"**: (0,0.5,v92)(1.0,0.25,v80)(1.5,0.5,v88)(2.5,0.25,v78)(3.0,0.5,v90)(3.75,0.25,v82), high piano/organ, straight. Ex: Versace.
- **TR-C5 "Toll"**: (0,1.0,v96)(1.0,1.0,v88)(2.0,1.0,v92)(3.0,1.0,v86) quarter-note bell chords. Ex: Lemonade.
### 2.4 Bass (the 808 lane — bassStyle=glide808)
- **TR-B1 "Hold + glide"**: (0, 3.5, R) with pitch glide beginning 3.0 → next chord's R (portamento 80-120ms). Follows chord roots exactly.
- **TR-B2 "Stutter"**: (0,0.25,R)(0.25,0.25,R)(0.5,0.5,R)(1.5,2.0,R); vel flat 110-115.
- **TR-B3 "Octave drop"**: (0,1.5,R)(1.5,0.5,R+oct)(2.5,1.5,b6 of key → glide to R).
- **TR-B4 "Roll-in"**: bar-tail 16th pickups (3.5,0.25,5)(3.75,0.25,b7) into next bar's R.
- Register C1-C2; 808 doubles the kick pattern 1:1 (kick trigger = 808 onset); decay 1-2 beats; MUST be tuned to key.
### 2.5 Melodic hook grammar
- Scales: minor pent (flute/guitar hooks), natural minor (bells), harmonic minor for arp menace, phrygian b2 accent. Range narrow: 4th-6th.
- Cells: t1 [(0,0.5),(0.5,0.5),(1.0,1.0)]; t2 triplet stamp [(0,0.33),(0.33,0.33),(0.67,0.33),(1.0,1.0)]; t3 tolls [(0,0.5),(1.0,0.5),(2.0,0.5),(3.0,0.5)]; t4 run-to-hold [(0,0.25),(0.25,0.25),(0.5,0.25),(0.75,0.25),(1.0,1.5)]; t5 back-half answer [(2.0,0.5),(2.5,0.5),(3.0,1.0)]; t6 long-tone [(0,1.0),(1.5,0.5),(2.0,2.0)]; t7 sparse [(0,0.5),(2.5,0.5)].
- Contour: descending or oscillating around 1/b3; ends on 1 or 5. Repetition: 1-bar cell x8 or 2-bar x4, ZERO variation (repetition is the aesthetic); leave beats 3-4 empty every other bar for ad-lib space. Register C5-C7 (bells), C4-C5 (flute/pluck). Hook length 2 bars. 808 glides act as second melody below.
- Original examples: **TR-H1** [(0,0.5,5'),(0.5,0.5,b3'),(1.0,1.0,1'),(2.0,0.5,b6'),(2.5,1.5,5')]. **TR-H2 triplet** [(0,0.33,1'),(0.33,0.33,b3'),(0.67,0.33,1'),(1.0,1.0,b7),(2.0,2.0,1)]. **TR-H3 toll** [(0,1.0,1'),(1.0,1.0,b6),(2.0,1.0,5),(3.0,1.0,b7)].
### 2.6 Instrumentation
- 808: GM38 Synth Bass 1, pitch-bend range 12, long release. Bells: GM14 tubular / GM9 glockenspiel / GM98 crystal / GM10 music box. Pads: GM89 warm / GM91 choir / GM52 choir aahs. Plucks: GM45 pizzicato, GM104 sitar-ish. Flute: GM73. Strings: GM48/50.
- Drums: kick sparse syncopated (e.g. 0, 1.75, 3.25), clap/snare only at 2.0, hats straight 16ths v60-85 with 32nd rolls (8 hits over 1 beat, vel ramp 60→110) at bar 4/8 tails + occasional triplet-substitution beats; open hat at 1.5 or 3.5.
### 2.7 Arrangement
- 8-bar sections. Intro 4-8 = melody solo (no drums); "drop" = kick+808 enter together; hook 8 / verse 16; mute 808 for 2 bars pre-drop; beat-switch (full new loop) as premium move.
- Hat-pattern density = the energy dial: verse 16ths, hook adds rolls + open hats.
### 2.8 Corny traps
- Untuned 808 → instantly fake. Fix: 808 root-locked to chord, glide only chord-root→chord-root.
- Hats with no rolls/velocity → drum machine demo. Fix: ≥1 roll per 2 bars, vel ramp inside rolls, 10-20% triplet substitution.
- I–V–vi–IV pop loop → not trap. Fix: only families in 2.2; keep b6/b2 colors.
- Busy hook → fake. Fix: ≤4 distinct pitches, cell repeats verbatim, 2-beat silence gaps.
- Snare on beats 1&3 (both backbeats) → boom-bap feel. Fix: single clap at 2.0 (half-time).
- Marcato strings playing melodies → epic-trailer corn. Fix: strings only as pads/1-hit stabs.
### 2.9 PD lifts (classical minor canon — trap's actual quarry today)
- Beethoven — Moonlight Sonata mvt 1 arpeggio (1802): ready-made i-arp over 808. Chopin — Prelude Op. 28 No. 4 (1839): descending-chord lament. Grieg — In the Hall of the Mountain King (1875): rising-cell menace. Vivaldi — Winter mvt 1 (1725). Bizet — Habanera from Carmen (1875): chromatic descent hook. Mozart — Lacrimosa (1791). Bach — Toccata in D minor (~1704). Dies Irae plainchant (trad): 4-note doom motif.
- Trap to flag: Orff's O Fortuna is 1936 — NOT PD. Use urtext scores, not modern editions/recordings.

## 3. G-FUNK — 88-96 BPM, swung 16ths ~56-58%, whiny lead + rubber synth bass
### 3.1 Canon
- Ohio Players — Funky Worm (1972): THE whiny portamento lead source (high sine/saw "worm").
- Parliament — Flash Light (1977): Moog-bass-as-lead root source; one-chord funk vamp.
- Parliament — Mothership Connection (1975): P-Funk chassis Dre rebuilt for Let Me Ride.
- Zapp — More Bounce to the Ounce (1980): talkbox + electro bounce; the West-coast groove floor.
- Dr. Dre — Nuthin' but a 'G' Thang (1992): two-chord minor vamp (from Leon Haywood), lazy swung hats, lead answers vocal.
- Dr. Dre — Let Me Ride (1992): P-Funk interpolation, whistle lead held over vamp.
- Dr. Dre & Snoop — Deep Cover (1992): 2-note sub ostinato menace archetype.
- Snoop Dogg — Gin and Juice (1993): bright dorian bounce, high sine lead hook.
- Warren G & Nate Dogg — Regulate (1994): smooth min9/maj7 quiet-storm loop (from Michael McDonald's "I Keep Forgettin'").
- 2Pac — California Love (1995): talkbox chorus hook (Roger Troutman), piano riff stabs.
- 2Pac — To Live & Die in L.A. (1996): mid-tempo dorian warmth.
- Ice Cube — It Was a Good Day (1992): maj7 soul loop (Isley "Footsteps in the Dark" lineage) — the mellow pole.
- DJ Quik — Tonite (1991): live-band funk precision, clav comping.
- Dr. Dre — Still D.R.E. (1999): staccato repeated high piano triads — the stab-comp archetype.
- Nate Dogg hooks generally: melodic baritone call-response = the vocal-hook grammar the lead imitates.
### 3.2 Harmony
- Families: i9–iv9 dorian vamp (1-2 bars each) ~40%; i7–bVII; i–bVI–bVII; 2-note-bass static i (Deep Cover pole); quiet-storm borrow: im9–bVImaj7 or maj9/min9 alternation (Good Day / Regulate pole).
- Dorian default; the 9th is near-mandatory color in both comp and lead; 11 on iv chords; blue b5 in lead fills.
- Two aesthetic poles to encode: "menace" (bare minor, 2 chords max) vs "smooth" (min9/maj7, 4 chords). Harmonic rhythm 1-2 bars/chord, 2- or 4-bar loop; riff-over-static with more chord color than trap.
### 3.3 Comping
- **GF-C1 "Upbeat skank"**: (1.5,0.25,v95)(3.5,0.25,v95) clav/keys rootless 3-b7-9. Ex: G Thang keys.
- **GF-C2 "Funk clav 16ths"**: (0,0.25,v100)(0.75,0.25,v85)(1.5,0.25,v95)(2.25,0.25,v85)(3.0,0.25,v98)(3.75,0.25,v80), swing 57%. Ex: Tonite.
- **GF-C3 "Still-style stab train"**: 8th-note staccato high triads, hits 0..3.5 step 0.5, dur 0.2, vel alternating 96/78; chord change per bar. Ex: Still D.R.E.
- **GF-C4 "Warm pad"**: (0, 4.0, v70) 9th-voiced pad, slow LPF sweep over 4 bars. Ex: Regulate bed.
- **GF-C5 "Talkbox answer"**: (2.5, 1.5, v88) sustained vocal-synth chord in bar tail, answers the lead.
### 3.4 Bass
- **GF-B1 "Moog syncopated walk"**: (0,0.5,R)(0.75,0.25,R)(1.5,0.5,5)(2.25,0.25,b7)(3.0,0.5,R)(3.5,0.5,5 below); swing 57%; D1-D2. Ex: G Thang lineage.
- **GF-B2 "Two-note menace"**: (0,1.0,R)(2.5,1.5,b3); sub register; vel 105 flat. Ex: Deep Cover archetype.
- **GF-B3 "Rubber slide"**: (0,2.0,R portamento-in from 5 below, 60ms)(2.5,1.0,b7)(3.5,0.5,R). Ex: Let Me Ride feel.
- **GF-B4 "Octave pop"**: (0,0.25,R)(0.5,0.25,R+oct)(1.5,0.25,R)(2.0,0.25,R+oct)(3.0,0.5,b7); slap patch GM36. Ex: Zapp lineage.
- Round sine/Moog tone (GM38/39); portamento 40-80ms on marked slides; busier than trap, never busier than 6 hits/bar.
### 3.5 Melodic hook grammar
- Scales: minor pent + 9 (the added 2 is THE g-funk lead color); blue b5 passing; dorian 6 in smooth mode. Lead = "whiny worm": every phrase-initial note approached by slide from 1-2 semitones below (portamento 30-80ms).
- Cells: g1 slide-hold [(0,0.5),(0.75,1.25)]; g2 [(0,0.25),(0.5,0.25),(1.0,0.5),(1.75,1.0)]; g3 answer [(2.0,0.5),(2.75,0.25),(3.0,1.0)]; g4 whistle hold [(0,3.0)] w/ vibrato 5-6Hz; g5 lazy quarters [(0,0.5),(1.0,0.5),(2.0,0.5),(3.0,1.0)]; g6 turnaround [(0,0.5),(0.5,0.5),(1.5,0.5),(2.5,1.5)].
- Contour: arch (rise then fall), phrase-final lands on 5 or 9(2); 2-bar phrases in call-response with the (imagined) vocal — melody plays bar 2 tails and bar 4. Register C5-C6, whistle variant C6-C7. Hook length 2-4 bars.
- Original examples: **GF-H1** [(0,0.5,1'),(0.75,1.25,2' slide-in),(2.5,0.5,b3'),(3.0,1.0,2')]. **GF-H2** [(0,0.25,5),(0.5,0.25,b7),(1.0,2.0,1' slide-in),(3.0,1.0,b7)]. **GF-H3 whistle** [(0,3.0,5') vibrato,(3.0,1.0,4'→b3' fall)].
### 3.6 Instrumentation
- Lead: GM80 square or GM85 voice lead, portamento ON, mono, pitch-bend for slides; whistle = GM80 two octaves up. Chords: GM7 clav, GM4 Rhodes, GM89 warm pad, GM50 synth strings. Bass: GM38/39 (round). Talkbox fake: GM85 short notes.
- Drums: fat kick 0 and 2.5-ish, layered snare+clap at 1.0/3.0 v110, swung 16th hats 57% with open hat at 3.5; occasional live-feel fills. Pack mapping: bassStyle=rootDriven or walking; swing 0.57.
### 3.7 Arrangement
- Verse/chorus pop form over a 2-4 bar loop. Lead plays only in vocal gaps during verses (bar tails), goes continuous in chorus; talkbox/whistle = chorus signature.
- Intro 4-8 bars: bass + clav only; add pad at verse; strings/whistle at chorus; drop to bass+hats for verse 2's first 4 bars.
### 3.8 Corny traps
- No portamento on lead → fake immediately. Fix: 30-80ms glide into ≥70% of lead notes, mono legato.
- Straight hats → drum machine. Fix: 56-58% swing, hat vel alternation ≥20.
- Natural-minor scalar lead runs → wrong. Fix: minor pent + 9 only, ≤5 notes/phrase, arch contour.
- Static bass whole notes → trap bleed. Fix: 4-6 syncopated hits/bar, R-5-b7 vocabulary.
- Lead below C5 → loses "whine". Fix: keep lead C5-C7, thin patch, vibrato on holds ≥1 beat.
### 3.9 PD lifts
- Thin — the funk sources (P-Funk, Zapp, Ohio Players) are 1970s+ and NOT PD: idiom-only, never melodic lifts. Usable PD: trad gospel/blues for lead quotes — Swing Low Sweet Chariot; When the Saints Go Marching In (pub. 1927); Frankie and Johnny (trad); House of the Rising Sun (trad — the Animals ARRANGEMENT is protected); St. Louis Blues (1914) bent-3rd vocabulary.

## 4. GRIME — 138-142 BPM (canon 140), straight 8th grid, 8-bar phrase law
### 4.1 Canon
- Wiley — Eskimo (2002, prod. ~1999-2000): eski square-bass riff, hollow percussion; the founding instrumental.
- Musical Mob — Pulse X (2002): 4-hit square sub, near-empty arrangement; "year zero for instrumental grime"; 138 BPM.
- Wiley — Igloo (2002) / Ice Rink (2003): eskibeat cold-synth palette, riff-switch structure.
- Dizzee Rascal — I Luv U (2003): string-slam stabs + sub; abrasive minimalism.
- Dizzee Rascal — Stop Dat (2003): shout-stab rhythm as hook.
- Danny Weed — Creeper (2002): cello ostinato riff — proof acoustic timbre works at 140.
- Ruff Sqwad — Functions on the Low (prod. XTC, 2004): wistful melodic square lead — the "beautiful grime" pole; later bed for Stormzy — Shut Up (2015).
- Ruff Sqwad — Pied Piper (2005): trance-adjacent square lead + pad lift.
- Lethal Bizzle — Pow! (Forward) (2004, Dexplicit): riff aggression ceiling, bass octave jumps.
- Kano — P's and Q's (2004): guitar-ish riff loop over 8-bar cycles.
- Jme — 96 Fuckries (2012): mid-era minimal square hook.
- Skepta — That's Not Me (2014): revival minimalism, 2-note bass hook.
- Skepta — Shutdown (2015): modern slam stabs + space.
- Stormzy — Shut Up (2015): melodic pole mainstreamed.
- Treble Clef — Ghetto Kyote (2004): mournful lead melody riddim — melody-as-riddim culture.
### 4.2 Harmony
- Frequently monophonic: harmony IS the riff. When chords appear: i–bVI string slams (2-4 bars each), i–bII phrygian slams, or static i with b2/b6 neighbors in the riff.
- No 7th/9th color — bare octaves, 5ths, minor triads; tritone and b2 dissonance welcome. Melodic pole (Functions type): aeolian 4-chord loops i–bVI–bIII–bVII allowed, still riff-led.
- Harmonic rhythm: static per 8-bar phrase; riff SWITCHES to new material (not variation) at 8-bar boundaries — structure A8 B8 A8 C8. Entirely riff-over-static.
### 4.3 Comping
- **GR-C1 "String slam"**: (0, 0.5, v112) per bar, octave+5th voicing, hard attack. Ex: I Luv U, Shutdown.
- **GR-C2 "8th square pulse"**: hits 0..3.5 step 0.5, dur 0.25, vel 90 flat; drop bars 7-8 of each phrase. Ex: Pulse X energy.
- **GR-C3 "Anticipation stab"**: (3.75, 0.25, v105) pickup slam into the next bar.
- **GR-C4 "Trance pad lift"**: (0, 4.0, v75) saw pad, B-sections only. Ex: Pied Piper.
### 4.4 Bass (bass and riff are often the SAME square patch, octave apart)
- **GR-B1 "Eski riff-bass"**: (0,0.5,R)(0.5,0.5,R+oct)(1.5,0.5,b3)(2.0,0.5,R)(3.0,0.5,b2)(3.5,0.5,R); staccato square; C2-C3 with octave jumps. Ex: Eskimo lineage.
- **GR-B2 "Pulse gut-punch"**: (0,0.75,R)(1.0,0.75,R)(2.0,0.75,R)(3.0,0.75,R) square sub, vel 115, R only. Ex: Pulse X.
- **GR-B3 "Wobble hold"**: (0, 4.0, R) with square LFO at 1/8 rate (gate feel).
- **GR-B4 "2-note hook bass"**: (0,0.5,R)(2.5,0.5,b6) x4 bars. Ex: That's Not Me minimalism.
- Register C1-C3 — grime bass JUMPS octaves (vs trap's sub-only); straight 8ths, no swing, no glide culture.
### 4.5 Melodic hook grammar
- Scales: minor pent + b2 (phrygian menace); melodic pole uses full natural minor. Square-wave mono lead; rests are punctuation — phrases END on rests, not resolutions.
- Cells: q1 [(0,0.5),(0.5,0.5),(1.5,0.5),(2.0,0.5),(3.0,0.5)]; q2 [(0,0.25),(0.5,0.25),(1.0,0.25),(1.5,0.25),(2.0,1.0)]; q3 [(0,0.5),(1.0,0.5),(1.75,0.25),(2.5,0.5),(3.5,0.5)]; q4 [(0,1.0),(2.0,0.5),(2.5,0.5),(3.0,1.0)]; q5 pickup [(3.5,0.5)] into next bar; q6 sparse slam [(0,0.5),(2.0,0.5)].
- Contour: zigzag with octave displacement (leaps of 8ve and 5th normal); b2→1 semitone cell = signature menace. Repetition: 1- or 2-bar riff x8 bars EXACTLY, then switch riffs entirely. Register C3-C5 (low for a lead — it doubles as bass). Straight 8ths/16ths, zero swing. Hook length = the whole 8-bar phrase.
- Original examples: **GR-H1** [(0,0.5,1),(0.5,0.5,1'),(1.5,0.5,b2'),(2.0,0.5,1'),(3.0,0.5,b3'),(3.5,0.5,1')]. **GR-H2** [(0,0.25,5),(0.5,0.25,5),(1.0,0.5,b6),(2.0,0.5,5),(2.75,0.25,4),(3.0,1.0,b3)]. **GR-H3 melodic pole** [(0,1.0,5'),(1.5,0.5,4'),(2.0,1.0,b3'),(3.5,0.5,4')] over i–bVI.
### 4.6 Instrumentation
- Riff/lead: GM80 square (canonical) or GM81 saw; bass GM39 synth bass 2 (harsh) or same GM80 down an octave. Stabs: GM48 strings, GM61 brass. Pads: GM50. Cello riff variant: GM42.
- Drums: kick 0 + 1.75 + 2.5 (broken 2-step DNA), snare/clap 1.0 and 3.0 (displace the second to 3.25 for broken feel), hats sparse straight 8ths v70; hollow/clicky kit, no 32nd rolls.
### 4.7 Arrangement
- 8-bar law: every 8 bars something switches (MC trade convention). A8 B8 A8 C8; intro = riff alone 8; "devil mix" section = drums+bass only.
- Strings/pad enter at bar 17-24 for lift; total loop material = 2-3 distinct riffs.
### 4.8 Corny traps
- Wrong tempo (128 or 174) → house/DnB. Fix: 138-142 only.
- Swing added → wrong nation. Fix: dead-straight grid, vel flat ±10.
- 4-bar loop repeated 16x → not grime. Fix: hard riff switch every 8 bars.
- Smooth timbre (sine/piano lead) → loses menace. Fix: square/saw, staccato 0.2-0.3 durs, octave jumps.
- Adding 7th/9th chords → jazz bleed. Fix: octaves, 5ths, bare minor triads only.
### 4.9 PD lifts
- Grime's string-stab culture takes classical menace well: Grieg — Mountain King (1875); Chopin — Funeral March (1839); Dies Irae chant (trad); Bach — Toccata in D minor; trad dorian folk — Scarborough Fair, Coventry Carol (both trad; specific modern arrangements protected). Flag: O Fortuna (1936) NOT PD despite feeling trad.

## 5. LO-FI HIP-HOP — 70-90 BPM, swing 56-62%, jazz harmony, dusty timing
### 5.1 Canon
- Nujabes — Aruarian Dance (2004): maj7 guitar cycle (sampled Laurindo Almeida's "The Lamp Is Low", itself from Ravel's Pavane, 1899) — the harmonic template.
- Nujabes — Feather (2005): piano ii-V loops + soft break.
- Nujabes — Luv(sic) Pt. 3 (2005): bright maj9 optimism pole.
- J Dilla — Donuts (2006, e.g. Don't Cry / Time: The Donut of the Heart): chopped-soul haze, off-grid placement — lo-fi's timing bible.
- Slum Village — Players (1997, Dilla): muted Rhodes shells, ghost bass.
- Uyama Hiroto — Waltz for Life Will Born (2008): Nujabes-school sax lyricism.
- Tomppabeats — Harbor (2016): 2-chord Rhodes loop minimalism.
- Jinsang — Affection (2016): kalimba/EP pentatonic hooks.
- Idealism — Snowfall (2017): maj9 melancholy, tape wobble.
- Saib — Sailing (2017): jazz-guitar comping loops.
- potsu — just friends (2017): jazzy ii-V bounce + vocal chop.
- Joey Pecoraro — Finding Parking (2017): humor-toned piano loop.
- eevee — seasons (2016): drifting EP + vinyl noise.
- DJ Okawari — Flower Dance (2010): piano-led melodic pole.
- Lofi Girl playlist canon at large (2017-): codified the 4-chord maj9/min9 2-4 bar loop + rain-noise aesthetic.
### 5.2 Harmony (the progression-driven member of this cluster)
- Families: ii7–V7–Imaj7(–vi7); stepwise walk IVmaj7–iii7–ii7–Imaj7; im9–iv9 dorian pair; Imaj9–vi9–ii9–V13; borrowed iv (Imaj7–ivm7); backdoor bVII9→Imaj7; secondary V7/ii; occasional tritone-sub bII7→I.
- EVERY chord carries min 7th; 9ths standard; 13 on V; sus4→3 inner motion on held chords. Major-key melancholy (maj7+9) ≈ 60%, dorian minor ≈ 40%.
- Cadences softened: ii-V lands on Imaj9 (never bare I); plagal ivm→I as outro move. Harmonic rhythm: 1 bar/chord (2 chords in bar 4 as turnaround); 4- or 8-bar loop cycling the entire track.
### 5.3 Comping
- **LF-C1 "Backbeat shimmer"**: (1.0,0.75,v72)(3.0,0.75,v68); rootless 9th voicings (3-5-7-9), register C3-C4; swing 58%.
- **LF-C2 "Push"**: (0,1.5,v75)(2.5,0.5,v65)(3.75,0.75,v70 — NEXT chord early, tied over barline). The anticipated-change move; neo-soul signature.
- **LF-C3 "Two-hand drift"**: (0,2.0,v70)(2.0,0.5,v60)(2.75,1.25,v66); ±25ms jitter; Rhodes. Ex: Dilla/Slum Village feel.
- **LF-C4 "Guitar cycle"**: (0,1.0)(1.5,1.0)(3.0,1.0) v65-75, arpeggiated maj7 (spread 0-2-4 semiquaver stagger). Ex: Aruarian Dance, Sailing.
- **LF-C5 "Bar 4 turnaround"**: normal pattern bars 1-3, then (0,1.0)(1.0,1.0)(2.0,2.0) with 2 chords in the bar.
### 5.4 Bass
- **LF-B1 "Two-feel"**: (0,1.5,R)(2.0,1.5,5); whole line +15-30ms late; GM32/33; A1-A2.
- **LF-B2 "Boom-bap borrow"**: (0,0.75,R)(2.5,0.5,R)(3.25,0.5,chrom→next R); vel 70-90.
- **LF-B3 "Melodic tail"**: (0,1.0,R)(2.0,0.5,5)(3.0,0.5,6)(3.5,0.5,b7) walking up into the change.
- Never slap, never sub-heavy; chromatic approach on the last 8th before a chord change is the genre's best cheap trick.
### 5.5 Melodic hook grammar
- Scales: major pent / dorian; but the REAL rule is chord-tone targeting — phrase endpoints land on the CURRENT chord's 3rd, 7th, or 9th (color tones), never the root. ≥50% of bars silent; phrases start OFF the downbeat.
- Cells: l1 [(0.5,0.5),(1.0,1.0)]; l2 [(1.5,0.5),(2.0,0.5),(2.5,1.5)]; l3 [(0,0.75),(1.0,0.5),(2.0,2.0)]; l4 pickup [(2.5,0.5),(3.0,0.5),(3.5,0.5 tie→next bar)]; l5 [(0.75,0.25),(1.0,0.5),(1.75,0.25),(2.0,1.0)]; l6 = play 1 bar, rest 1 bar (mandatory scheme, not optional).
- Contour: gentle descent or sigh (up a step, down a third); range ≤ octave. Repetition: 2-bar phrase, answered by varied 2-bar phrase (period form), whole 4-bar unit x2 then rest 4. Register C4-C5, just above comp. Articulation: legato, vel 55-80, +20ms behind beat; approximate slides with grace note 1 semitone below, 60ms.
- Original examples (major-key degrees): **LF-H1** over Imaj7: [(0.5,0.5,3'),(1.0,0.5,2'),(1.5,1.0,7),(3.0,1.0,6)]. **LF-H2** over ii7→V7: [(1.5,0.5,4),(2.0,0.5,3),(2.5,1.5,2)] → next bar [(0,2.0,5)]. **LF-H3 kalimba**: [(0,0.5,5),(0.75,0.25,6),(1.5,0.5,3),(2.5,1.0,2)].
### 5.6 Instrumentation
- Chords: GM4 Rhodes / GM5 EP2 / GM26 jazz guitar / GM0 piano (detune −10 cents, LPF ~6kHz to fake tape). Bass: GM32 upright or GM33 muted-feel. Melody: GM73 flute, GM11 vibes, GM66 tenor sax, GM108 kalimba, GM52 choir oohs.
- Drums: soft kick v88-100, snare v80-90 with rim layer, hats swung 56-60% v45-70, NO crashes; kick at 0 and 2.75 typical; everything slightly late except kick.
- Pack mapping: bassStyle=walking; swing 0.58; melodyDensity LOW.
### 5.7 Arrangement
- One 4-8 bar loop = the whole track (90-150s). Energy via texture: drop drums for 1 bar every 8 ("beat tag"), mute bass for intro 4, add melody only in the middle third, filter-close the outro.
- No verse/chorus — form is intro(4) / body(24-48) / outro(4-8).
### 5.8 Corny traps
- Triads → instantly not lo-fi. Fix: min 7th on every chord, 9ths preferred, rootless above C3.
- Full quantization → sterile. Fix: swing 56-60% + per-lane lateness (melody +20ms, snare +10ms), vel jitter ±10.
- Continuous 8th-note melody → music-box corn. Fix: l6 rule (≥50% rest bars), phrase starts off the downbeat.
- Loud/bright mix of velocities → wrong energy. Fix: cap all vel ≤ 100; melody 55-80.
- Root-landing melodies → nursery feel. Fix: end phrases on 3/7/9 of current chord.
- Random "jazz" chords → aimless. Fix: only the 5.2 families; keep the bass walking to the change.
### 5.9 PD lifts (richest of the cluster)
- Satie — Gymnopédie No. 1 (1888) + Gnossienne No. 1 (1890): already lo-fi harmonically. Debussy — Clair de Lune (1905), Rêverie (1890), The Girl with the Flaxen Hair (1910). Ravel — Pavane pour une infante défunte (1899): the verified Nujabes lineage — lift Ravel directly. Chopin — Nocturne Op. 9 No. 2 (1832). Joplin — Solace (1909). Tchaikovsky — June Barcarolle (1876). Early standards now PD: Someone to Watch Over Me (1926), Stardust (music 1927).
- Traps: "The Lamp Is Low" (1939 song built ON the Pavane) is NOT PD — go to Ravel's original. Modern editions/recordings of all the above carry their own copyrights; work from urtext/first-edition pitch content.
