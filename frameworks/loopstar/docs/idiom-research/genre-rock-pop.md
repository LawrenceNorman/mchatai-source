# Idiom Source-Book: Rock / Pop / Punk / Metal / Folk
For LoopStar material-pack authors. Conventions: 4/4, onsets `(beat,dur)` at 16th resolution (0.25), beats 0–4; vel 0–127; swing % (50=straight, 58≈light shuffle, 62=hard shuffle applied to off-8ths). Melody = key scale degrees (1..7, b3, b7; `'`=octave up, `,`=octave down). Bass = chord-relative: R, 3, 5, 6, b7, O(octave), C↑/C↓(chromatic approach), X(dead note vel≤40). GM programs 1-based (1=Acoustic Grand). Drum notes: K=36 S=38 HH=42 OH=46 Ride=51 Crash=49 Clap=39 Tamb=54. Bend/slide approximation: grace note 1–2 semitones below target, onset −0.08 beats, dur 0.1, vel −15, overlapping the target note.
**PD legal frame (verified 2026):** US public domain = works PUBLISHED through 1930; sound recordings through 1925. Melody+changes of the original sheet are free; famous later recordings and arrangements are separately copyrighted. Non-US: life+70 mostly.

## A. ROCK (classic + indie riffcraft)
**1. CANON** — Chuck Berry–Johnny B. Goode (straight-8 I-IV-V boogie comp; double-stop lead vocab) · Kinks–You Really Got Me (2-chord power riff shifted a whole step; riff=song) · Stones–Satisfaction (3-note b7-centered fuzz hook) · Cream–Sunshine of Your Love (minor-pent riff w/ b5 over static harmony) · Led Zeppelin–Whole Lotta Love (1-bar E-pent riff ×4 over pedal E; slide into b7) · Deep Purple–Smoke on the Water (parallel-4ths riff, G minor pent) · Free–All Right Now (gap comp: stabs + silence) · AC/DC–Back in Black (rest-driven riff, E5-D5-A5 + pent fill) · T. Rex–Get It On (boogie shuffle riff, swing 8ths) · Television–Marquee Moon (interlocking clean single-note riffs = indie blueprint) · Pixies–Where Is My Mind? (loud-quiet-loud arrangement) · Nirvana–Smells Like Teen Spirit (4 power-chord loop i-iv-bIII-bVI; quiet verse/loud chorus) · R.E.M.–The One I Love (jangle arpeggio comp, add9 open strings) · Strokes–Last Nite (flat-velocity downstroke-8ths comp) · White Stripes–Seven Nation Army (low-register natural-minor descending hook, bass-as-lead) · QOTSA–No One Knows (syncopated dorian riff w/ stop-gaps) · Arctic Monkeys–Do I Wanna Know? (half-time stomp + unison minor-pent riff) · Black Keys–Lonely Boy (one-riff blues-rock, mixo/dorian blend).
**2. HARMONY** — Two engines: (a) riff-over-static: one pent riff loops 1–2 bars over pedal I for 4–8 bars (Zep/QOTSA/Keys); (b) power-chord loops 1–2 chords/bar. Families: I-IV-V (12-bar, dom7s); I-bVII-IV(-I) mixolydian; i-bIII-bVII-IV dorian rock; i-bVI-bVII aeolian; I-V-vi-IV pop-rock; i-iv-bIII-bVI grunge. bVII and bIII borrowed into major everywhere. Power chords omit the 3rd → modal ambiguity is a feature; the MELODY supplies b3-vs-3 (blue zone). Extensions: dom7 on I/IV/V blues only; add9/sus2 on clean-jangle comp; sus4→3 resolution on sustains (Stones/Who move). Cadences: bVII-I and IV-I; V-I mainly inside blues. Harmonic rhythm: verse 1 chord/1–2 bars, chorus doubles rate.
**3. COMPING** — (1) *Downstroke-8s wall* (Strokes, Petty): onsets 0,0.5..3.5, dur .4, vel 108 on-beats/96 offs, swing 50, power/barre A2–A3. (2) *Boogie pump* (Berry, Quo): same grid but dyad alternates R+5↔R+6 each 8th-pair; swing 50 (50s) to 62 (glam). (3) *Gap-stab* (AC/DC, Free): (0,.5,115)(0.75,.25,110)(1.5,1,118) then SILENCE to pickup (3.5,.5,95); staccato ≤.5 except the 1.5 hit rings. (4) *Jangle arpeggio* (R.E.M., Smiths): single notes 8ths cycling R-5-O-3-9-O, dur 1.5–2 (let ring), vel 84±6, add9 tone every cycle. (5) *Push change*: comp 8ths but next chord arrives at 3.5 of the prior bar, tied through 0. (6) *Grunge toggle*: verse = (0,4) whole-bar power chord vel 70 clean; chorus = 8ths wall vel 120 distorted.
**4. BASS** — (1) *8ths lock*: R×8, dur .45, vel 100, GM35 pick, mirrors comp grid exactly. (2) *Boogie line*: 8ths R-3-5-6 up, b7-6-5-3 down per chord, swing matches gtr. (3) *Riff unison* (highest impact; Zep/QOTSA): double the guitar riff 1 octave down, identical grid. (4) *R-5 quarters*: (0,R)(1,5)(2,R)(3,5) vel 95, heartland/early rock'n'roll. (5) *Chromatic approach*: R sustain 0–3, then (3.5,.25)(3.75,.25) C↑ or C↓ into next root. (6) *Tonic pedal*: key-R 8ths while chords move I-bVII-IV above (Who-style tension). Register E1–A2; vel jitter ±8.
**5. HOOK GRAMMAR** — Scales: minor pent + b5; major pent (bright classic); mixolydian b7 over major; dorian for indie riffs; alternate b3↔3 in one phrase (blue 3rd). Register: riffs E2–E3 (below comp), leads/hooks C4–A5 (above). Repetition: riff ×4 with bar-4 turnaround variant; or call(2 bars)+response(2). Hook length 1–2 bars. Contours: descend b3→1 or 5→1; V-shape (1→b3→1); 30% of riffs start on a 3.5 pickup in the prior bar. Cells: C1 (0,.5)(.5,.5)(1,.5)(1.5,.5)(2,1)(3,1); C2 gap (0,.25)(.75,.25)(1.5,.5)(3.5,.5); C3 triplet-drag pickup (3,.33)(3.33,.33)(3.66,.34); C4 pump 8ths ×8; C5 sync (0,.75)(.75,.75)(1.5,.5)(2.5,.5)(3,.5)(3.5,.5); C6 stop-riff (0,.5)(.5,.25)(.75,.25)(1,.5) rest to 4; C7 shuffle 8ths swing 62; C8 held-hook (0,3)(3,.5)(3.5,.5). **Original hooks:** H1 riff (minor): 1(0,.5) 1(.5,.25) b3(.75,.25) 4(1,.5) b5(1.5,.25) 4(1.75,.25) b3(2,.5) 1(2.5,1.5). H2 lead (mixo-blues): 5(0,.75) b7(.75,.25) 1'(1,1) b7(2,.5) 5(2.5,.5) 4(3,.5) b3(3.5,.5). H3 jangle (major pent): 3(0,.5) 2(.5,.5) 1(1,.5) 5(1.5,1) 6(2.5,.5) 5(3,1).
**6. INSTRUMENTATION** — Lead GM30 Overdriven (blues-rock) / GM31 Distortion (heavy). Comp GM30, or GM28 Clean for jangle/indie; acoustic layer GM26; organ pad GM19 vel 70 sustained. Bass GM35 (GM34 for 60s–70s finger). Drums: S on 1,3; HH 8ths verse → Ride/Crash chorus; 16th-snare fill beats 3–4 of bar 4. Power-chord voicing R+5+O only below C3; triads/barre above.
**7. ARRANGEMENT** — 4-bar riff loop is the atom. Intro (riff ×2, gtr alone) → verse 8 (drop to clean/1 gtr, bass enters) → chorus 8 (+crash, +2nd gtr octave) → riff → solo over verse changes → stop-time bar (single hit at 0, then silence) → double chorus. Loud-quiet-loud is THE lever.
**8. CORNY TRAPS** — (1) Full triads w/ 3rd on distorted low gtr = mud → R+5(+O) below C3. (2) Unbroken 8ths = sewing machine → composed rests (gap-stab), vel accent map ±20. (3) Riffs always starting dead on 0 → add 3.5 pickups. (4) Chord-per-bar forever → riff-over-static 2–4 bars, vary harmonic rhythm per section. (5) Lead arpeggiates the chord → pent scale, target 3/b7 at changes, blue notes. (6) Crash every bar → crashes only at section heads.
**9. PD LIFTS** — "John Henry" (trad), "Stagger Lee" (pub. 1911; avoid Lloyd Price 1958 arr.), "St. Louis Blues" (Handy 1914, incl. its tango-vamp bass), "Careless Love" (trad), "Frankie and Johnny" (pub. 1912), "Midnight Special" (trad; Lead Belly/CCR arrs. ©), "In the Pines" (trad; Nirvana arr. ©), "When the Saints" (pub. 1896). The 12-bar form itself is free. Rule: lift the trad tune, never a famous recorded arrangement.

## B. POP (Motown → Max Martin)
**1. CANON** — Supremes–You Can't Hurry Love (pumping R-5-6 bass + tambourine drive) · Temptations–My Girl (backbeat gtr chank; major-pent bass walk-up as intro hook shape) · Jackson 5–I Want You Back (bassline-as-hook; gospel IV/5 chords; descending runs) · Stevie Wonder–Signed Sealed Delivered (syncopated stab comp) · ABBA–Dancing Queen (piano 8th pulse; descending inner-voice hooks) · Michael Jackson–Billie Jean (immutable minor 8th-ostinato bass under static i) · Madonna–Into the Groove (synth-bass 8ths + off-beat stabs) · Whitney–I Wanna Dance with Somebody (octave synth bass; IV-V pre-chorus lift) · a-ha–Take On Me (16th arpeggio synth hook >C5) · Britney–…Baby One More Time (3-note minor piano stab; Martin era opens) · Backstreet Boys–I Want It That Way (melodic-math AAB phrasing) · Kelly Clarkson–Since U Been Gone (verse restraint → octave-leap chorus) · Katy Perry–Teenage Dream (4-chord loop that never lands root-position I) · Taylor Swift–Blank Space (one-note verse melody vs moving chords) · Carly Rae Jepsen–Call Me Maybe (string-stab riff hook; pre-chorus cut to empty downbeat) · Robyn–Dancing On My Own (sad-banger loop, pedal bass) · Weeknd–Blinding Lights (saw-lead hook, straight-8 synth bass, zero swing) · Dua Lipa–Don't Start Now (disco octave-bass revival).
**2. HARMONY** — Families: Axis I-V-vi-IV + rotations (vi-IV-I-V, IV-I-V-vi); doo-wop I-vi-IV-V; Motown gospel I-iii-IV-V with IV/5 ("V11") as the pop dominant and ii7-V turnarounds; minor pop i-bVI-bIII-bVII, i-bVII-bVI; 80s mixo bVII in major. Martin era: ONE 4-chord loop all song; freshness from where the melody sits, not new chords; loops often avoid root-position tonic (start on IV or vi). Extensions: add9/sus2 default on piano/pads; maj7 = dreamy; dom7 = retro flag; melody carries 9ths/4ths over plain triads. Cadences: plagal IV-I, bVII-I; hard V7-I only as throwback; loops frequently cadence-free (tension via arrangement). Harmonic rhythm 1 chord/bar (or /2 bars); anticipate changes at 3.5; pre-chorus may double rate. De-stock tricks: rotate loop start off-tonic; swap ONE chord per section (vi→iii, IV→ii); verse pedal-bass under changing chords, release at chorus.
**3. COMPING** — (1) *Motown chank*: gtr chord (1,.15,112)(3,.15,112) only; tambourine 8ths; bass fills the space. (2) *Piano 8th pulse* (ABBA): RH add9 chords 8ths dur .45, vel 96/84 on/off; LH octave quarters. (3) *Off-beat stab* (dance-pop): (0.5,.2)(1.5,.2)(2.5,.2)(3.5,.2) vel 105, rootless 3-note voicings C4–C5. (4) *Push-pull stab* (R&B-pop): (0,.3)(0.75,.3)(1.5,.3)(2.5,.3)(3.75,.25 tied over barline). (5) *Pad+chank*: pad (0,4) vel 70 + chank on 1,3 — thin verse default. (6) *Stab-riff hook* (Call Me Maybe-style strings): quarters (0)(1)(2)(3) dur .2 vel 110, TOP NOTE moves stepwise = comp is the hook. Voicing rules: RH rootless (3-5-9 / 3-7-9) C4–C6; no doubled roots under the vocal; choose inversions so the top note moves stepwise (top-note voice-leading = pop sheen).
**4. BASS** — (1) *Jamerson busy* (GM34): R(0,.5) R(.75,.25) 5(1,.5) 6(1.5,.25) X(1.75,.25) R(2,.5) 5(2.75,.25) C↑(3.5,.5); ghost 16ths vel 35; chromatic approach into every chord. (2) *Octave disco*: 8ths R-O-R-O…, vel 100/88 (Dancing Queen, Don't Start Now). (3) *Minor ostinato loop* (Billie Jean family): 8ths R-5-b7-O-b7-5, NEVER varies, under static i, dur .4. (4) *Sidechain-fake* (synth GM39): 8ths all R, vel pairs 112/72, dur .35. (5) *Sustain+pickup*: R(0,3.25) + C↑ 16ths (3.5,.25)(3.75,.25). (6) *Straight-8 flat* (Blinding Lights): R×8 dur .45 vel 100, swing 50 exactly.
**5. HOOK GRAMMAR (melodic math, verified)** — Melody first; each line's syllable count mirrors the previous line; ≤3–4 distinct sections; chorus arrives <50s. Scales: major pent for chorus hooks; natural minor for moody pop. Repetition: **AAB'** — cell ×2 exact, 3rd time change ONLY the last 2–3 notes; chorus = 2-bar hook ×4 (micro-variation on the 4th). Intervals: mostly steps; ONE signature leap (4th/5th/octave) per section, placed at chorus start, then step back down. One-note-melody option: repeat 1̂ or 5̂ while chords move underneath. Register: chorus centroid a 4th–octave above verse; pre-chorus climbs stepwise. Cells: P1 (0,.25)(.25,.25)(.5,.5)(1,.5)(1.5,.5)(2,1.5); P2 off-start (0.5,.25)(.75,.25)(1,.5)(1.5,.5)(2.5,.5)(3,1); P3 8th-run 0–2 + (2,2) landing; P4 sync (0,.75)(.75,.75)(1.5,.75)(2.25,.75)(3,1); P5 stutter (0,.25)(.5,.25)(1,.25)(1.5,.25)(2,.5)(3,.5); P6 anacrusis (3,.5)(3.5,.5)→(0,1); P7 long-note (0,2.5)(2.5,.5)(3,1); P8 snap-pairs (0,.25)(.25,.75)(1,.25)(1.25,.75)(2,2). **Original hooks:** H1 (major, over I-V-vi-IV, AAB'): 3(0,.5) 3(.5,.5) 4(1,.5) 3(1.5,.5) 2(2,1) 5(3,1) ×2, third pass ends 2(2,.5) 1(2.5,1.5). H2 (minor, over i-bVI-bIII-bVII): 5(0,.75) 5(.75,.75) b6(1.5,.5) 5(2,.5) 4(2.5,.5) b3(3,1). H3 (one-note): 1'(0,.25)(.5,.25)(1,.25)(1.5,.25) 1'(2,.5) b7(2.5,.5) 1'(3,1) over the moving loop.
**6. INSTRUMENTATION** — Piano GM1; EP GM5 (R&B-lean); saw lead GM82, square GM81; pads GM89/GM92; string stabs GM49 (dur .2 vel 110); brass stabs GM62 (Motown); gtr chank GM28. Bass: GM34 Motown, GM39 modern synth, GM35 pop-rock. Drums: Motown = snare on ALL 4 beats or 1,3 + Tamb 8ths + Clap layered; dance-pop = K four-on-floor (0,1,2,3) + Clap 1,3 + OH on off-8ths; modern trap-pop = half-time S on 2 only + HH 16ths w/ vel drops.
**7. ARRANGEMENT** — V(8, thin: K+bass+sparse comp) → Pre(4–8, stepwise rising melody, build, CUT last half-bar to silence) → Chorus(8, full, hook ×4) → V2 (one layer less than chorus + new ear-candy) → Pre → Chorus → middle-8 (pedal or one new chord) → final Chorus (+octave ad-libs, +claps). Variants: drop-chorus (first chorus bar = vocal+bass only); post-chorus instrumental hook restating the chorus rhythm on a synth.
**8. CORNY TRAPS** — (1) Melody arpeggiates chords → sing 9/4/6 color tones, hold common tone across changes. (2) Root-position block triads in both hands → rootless RH, stepwise top note, bass owns roots. (3) Loop begins on root-position I → rotate to vi/IV, sus2 the tonic chord. (4) Hook varied every repeat (or stated once) → exact ×2, vary only the ending (AAB'). (5) No anticipation, flat vel → push chords at 3.5, backbeat accents. (6) V7→I everywhere → plagal/bVII or cadence-free loop.
**9. PD LIFTS** — Now shippable melodies (US, pub. ≤1930): "I Got Rhythm" (1930 — and rhythm-changes progression), "Georgia on My Mind" (1930), "Sweet Georgia Brown" (1925), "Bye Bye Blackbird" (1926), "Makin' Whoopee" (1928), "Yes Sir, That's My Baby" (1925), "Ain't We Got Fun" (1921), "Charleston" (1923), "After You've Gone" (1918), "Someone to Watch Over Me" (1926). TRAP: only original sheet melody/changes are PD — later famous recordings, added verses, and arrangements remain ©; sound recordings only PD through 1925.

## C. PUNK (8th-note drive)
**1. CANON** — Ramones–Blitzkrieg Bop (downstroke-8ths bed; "chant = 2-note hook") · Sex Pistols–God Save the Queen (mid-tempo swagger, barre chords) · Clash–White Riot (3-chord 190+ BPM sprint) · Clash–London Calling (melodic/reggae-tinged bass counter-line) · Buzzcocks–Ever Fallen in Love (pop melody grafted onto drive) · Damned–New Rose (drum-intro urgency, half-bar stops) · Stiff Little Fingers–Alternative Ulster (octave-lead anthem chorus) · Dead Kennedys–Holiday in Cambodia (surf-toned minor riff) · Misfits–Where Eagles Dare (whoa-oh gang-vocal grammar) · Black Flag–Rise Above (hardcore chug, shouted call-response) · Minor Threat–Straight Edge (230 BPM compression, 46-second form) · Descendents–Suburban Home (melodic hardcore: pop chords at punk tempo) · Bad Religion–American Jesus (3-part "oozin-aah" harmony pads over 8ths) · Green Day–Basket Case (palm-mute verse → open chorus; descending-bass pop-punk loop) · Offspring–Self Esteem (minor Axis i-bVI-bIII-bVII, la-la gang hook) · Blink-182–Dammit (4-chord rotation + melodic lead-in gtr riff) · Rancid–Time Bomb (ska upstroke + walking bass) · NOFX–Linoleum (skate-punk double-time, melodic bass intro).
**2. HARMONY** — 3–4 chords, majors: I-IV-V (+bVII); I-V-vi-IV and rotations (pop-punk default); minor anthem i-bVI-bIII-bVII; vi is the only minor chord used in major keys. Chromatic power-chord passing allowed (parallel 5ths are idiomatic). Keys E/A/G/D. NO extensions — power chords/barre majors only (one sus4 per song max as pre-chorus tension). Progression-driven, changes ON downbeats; 1 chord/bar verse → 2 chords/bar chorus (harmonic rhythm accelerates into chorus).
**3. COMPING** — (1) *Downstroke wall*: 8ths ×8, dur .42, vel 112 flat (±6 max), swing 50 ALWAYS, power chords A2–A3. (2) *Palm-mute verse*: same grid dur .18 vel 88; chorus flips to (1) at vel 118 — the genre's main dynamic lever. (3) *Ska upstroke*: (0.5,.15)(1.5,.15)(2.5,.15)(3.5,.15) vel 100, top-3-string voicings, pairs with walking bass. (4) *Stab+stop breakdown*: (0,.5)(2,.5) vel 120, silence between, drums keep 8ths. (5) *Octave-lead overlay* (SLF/Bad Religion): single-note octaves, 8ths, following vocal contour, vel 105, above the wall.
**4. BASS** — (1) *Root-8ths lock* (default): R×8 dur .4 vel 105 GM35; fills ONLY on last 2 8ths of a 4-bar phrase (C↑ walk to next root). (2) *Melodic counter-line* (Clash/NOFX): R-3-5-6 8th walks + stepwise runs linking chords, mixed forward. (3) *Ska walk*: quarters R(0) 3(1) 5(2) 6(3) per chord under upstrokes. (4) *Root-16ths surge* (hardcore chorus, only ≤180 BPM): R×16 dur .2 vel 100.
**5. HOOK GRAMMAR** — Chant-first. (a) Gang chants: 1–3 pitches from {1̂,5̂,6̂}, syllabic quarters/8ths, drop-contour (5→3, 1→1). (b) Whoa-oh vowels (Misfits): stepwise 5-6-5-3 / 1-2-3-2 shapes, held 8ths+quarters, gang unison. (c) Melodic-punk verse: major scale, range ≤6th, straight-8th syllabic strings ending on a long note; melisma banned. Hook 1–2 bars ×4; call(vocal 1 bar)+response(gtr/gang 1 bar). Cells: K1 8ths 0–3 + (3,1); K2 chant anacrusis (3,.5)(3.5,.5)→(0,1); K3 (0,.5)(.5,.5)(1,1)(2,.5)(2.5,.5)(3,1); K4 whoa (0,1)(1,.5)(1.5,.5)(2,2); K5 shout stabs (0,.5)(1.5,.5)(3,.5). **Original hooks:** H1 chant: 5(0,.5) 5(.5,.5) 6(1,.5) 5(1.5,.5) 3(2,1) 1(3,1). H2 whoa: 1'(0,1) b7(1,.5) 5(1.5,.5) 6(2,2). H3 verse cell: 3(0,.5) 3(.5,.5) 3(1,.5) 4(1.5,.5) 5(2,.5) 5(2.5,.5) 2(3,1).
**6. INSTRUMENTATION** — Gtr GM30 (UK/pop-punk) or GM31 (hardcore); GM28 only for ska upstrokes. Bass GM35. Drums vel 110–120 flat, zero ghost notes: punk beat = K(0,2) S(1,3) HH 8ths; D-beat = K(0,0.75,2,2.75) S(1,3); chorus = crash-ride 8ths on 49. Tempi: UK '77 150–180, pop-punk 155–185, skate 180–210, hardcore 190–230, ska-punk 130–180.
**7. ARRANGEMENT** — Count-in bar (4 rim clicks = "1-2-3-4!"). Total form ≤2:30: V8–C8–V8–C8–bridge/solo 4-8–C×2–cold end (single hit at 0, dur .5, silence). Energy levers: palm-mute↔open wall, one halt-bar (full stop, drum pickup at 3–4), gang-vox layer on final chorus. Intros ≤4 bars.
**8. CORNY TRAPS** — (1) Any swing or "humanize" → swing 50, hard quantize; sloppiness ≠ swing. (2) Funk ghost notes / busy bass fills → flat velocity, root lock. (3) 7ths/sus/jazz color → power chords only. (4) Pop-punk below 150 BPM reads as rock → 165+, double-time feel. (5) Verse energy = chorus energy → palm-mute or thin the verse, save the wall. (6) Melisma/R&B runs → syllabic only.
**9. PD LIFTS** (thin — genre post-1974; trad roots work as chants): "Drunken Sailor" (trad dorian shanty — natural over 8th-drive), "When the Saints" (1896), "My Bonnie" (1881), "Ta-Ra-Ra Boom-de-ay" (1891 chant chorus), "John Brown's Body" (1861 gang-chant contour). TRAP: modern punk covers of trad tunes are © arrangements — lift only the trad melody.

## D. METAL (chugs, gallops, harmonic minor)
**1. CANON** — Black Sabbath–Black Sabbath (1-b5 tritone doom riff) · Black Sabbath–Iron Man (chromatic power-chord climb over static i) · Judas Priest–Breaking the Law (aeolian riff + open-string pedal) · Motörhead–Ace of Spades (punk-metal 16th drive) · Iron Maiden–The Trooper (gallop + harmonized-3rds twin lead) · Ozzy–Crazy Train (natural-minor riff over pedal; pop-clear chorus) · Dio–Holy Diver (mid-tempo chug; i-bVII-bVI moves) · Metallica–Master of Puppets (downpicked E-pedal 16th chugs + chromatic chords) · Metallica–Enter Sandman (minor-pent riff w/ b5, arena groove) · Slayer–Raining Blood (tremolo picking, chromatic/diminished cells) · Megadeth–Symphony of Destruction (half-time stomp chug, phrygian b2) · Pantera–Walk (groove-metal triplet-lean syncopated chug) · Sepultura–Refuse/Resist (reverse gallop, tom groove) · At the Gates–Slaughter of the Soul (melodeath: aeolian twin leads over tremolo) · Meshuggah–Bleed (polymetric kick 8ths under steady 4/4 cymbals = djent blueprint) · Ghost–Square Hammer (pop-structured metal, major/minor mix).
**2. HARMONY** — Riff-over-static i dominates (E standard; drop-D/down-tune widens). Families: i-bII phrygian slide; i-bVI-bVII aeolian anthem (cadence bVI-bVII-i); i-bVII-bVI-V harmonic-minor cadence (neoclassical); i-bIII-bVII; tritone axis 1-b5 (doom); pure chromatic cells (roots moving by m2/m3). Power chords only (R+5, R+5+O). Modes: aeolian default; phrygian = menace; phrygian dominant (b2+nat3) = exotic cadence; harmonic minor for leads over V; dorian rare (groove). Diminished (b5 dyad) passing in thrash. Harmonic rhythm: riff = 1–2 bar loop ×4; chords change 2–4×/bar INSIDE the riff but tonal center static 8+ bars; chorus is often the first real root motion (to bVI or iv). No maj7, ever.
**3. RIFF/COMP PATTERNS** — (1) *Pedal chug 16ths* (Puppets): open-R 16ths dur .12 vel 92±5 palm-muted; power-chord accents replace 16ths at 0, 1.5, 2.5 (dur .3, vel 122); swing 50. (2) *Gallop* (Maiden): per beat (0,.5)(.5,.25)(.75,.25), muted dur .1, vel 100/90/90, ×4 beats. (3) *Reverse gallop* (Sepultura): per beat (0,.25)(.25,.25)(.5,.5). (4) *Doom sustain* (Sabbath, 58–75 BPM): (0,2.5)(2.5,.75)(3.25,.75) power chords vel 118, neighbor = b5 or b2; trill tail = two 16ths at 3.5. (5) *Breakdown chug*: (0,.2)(0.75,.2)(1.25,.2)(2,.2)(2.75,.2)(3.25,.2) all on R; kick mirrors EXACTLY; every 4th bar drop the beat-3 hits (silence = weight). (6) *Tremolo 16ths* (Slayer/black): single note ×16 dur .2 vel 86±8, root moves per half-bar chromatically or by 4ths.
**4. BASS** — (1) *Riff unison* octave down (default; identical grid). (2) *Root 8ths* under tremolo/leads. (3) *Harris gallop*: fingered trebly GM34, gallop grid on R + scalar fill (5-6-b7-O 16th run) on beat 3–4 of each 4-bar phrase. (4) *Breakdown sustain*: R dur 2–4, kick carries rhythm. Vel 110+.
**5. LEAD/HOOK GRAMMAR** — Scales: aeolian; minor pent + b5; phrygian; harmonic minor ONLY over V→i bars; phrygian dominant for exotic cadence bars. Twin lead: harmonize a diatonic 3rd (or 6th) above, identical grid. Vocal hook modes: thrash rhythmic monotone on 1̂ (rhythm IS the hook); power-metal soar (long 5̂-b6-5̂, octave leaps, dur ≥2); groove shout synced to chug rests. Riff registers E1–E3 (D1 down-tuned); leads E4–E6. Cells: M1 gallop ×3 beats + turn (3,.5)(3.5,.5); M2 pedal-return 16ths alternating open-R with rising chromatic uppers (R-b2-R-2-R-b3…); M3 tritone stab 1(0,1.5) b5(1.5,.5) 1(2,2); M4 aeolian 8th run 1-2-b3-2-b3-4-b3-2; M5 tremolo span: 16ths one degree per half-bar descending b6→5→4→b3; M6 rhythm-only breakdown cell (0,.25)(.75,.25)(1.75,.25)(2.5,.25)(3.25,.25) on 1̂. Articulation: pinch harmonic = grace +12/+19 semitones, vel 127, dur .1; wide vibrato → two tied notes vel ±25. **Original hooks:** H1 (aeolian riff): 1(0,.5) 1(.5,.25) 1(.75,.25) b3(1,.5) 2(1.5,.5) 1(2,.5) b7,(2.5,.5) 1(3,1). H2 (phrygian): 1(0,.75) b2(.75,.25) 1(1,.5) b3(1.5,.5) b2(2,.5) 1(2.5,1.5). H3 (harm-minor twin lead over V): 5(0,1) b6(1,.5) 5(1.5,.5) 4(2,.5) b3(2.5,.5) 2(3,.5) 7(3.5,.5)→1; add parallel voice a 3rd above.
**6. INSTRUMENTATION** — Rhythm GM31 (double the line at vel 115 for thickness); leads GM30/31 octave up; clean interlude GM28. Bass GM34/35. Drums: kick mirrors riff-onset grid (16th doubles); S(1,3) groove, S(2) only = half-time breakdown; blast = K/S alternating 16ths; china/crash at riff heads; ride-bell 8ths for groove. Tempi: doom 60–85, groove 85–110, classic 100–140, thrash 160–220, melodeath 180–240 (half-time feel).
**7. ARRANGEMENT** — Intro (clean arp or fade-in riff) 4–8 → Riff A ×4 (instrumental hook) → verse (vox over Riff A) → pre (new rising riff) → chorus (root motion opens up: bVI-bVII-i; longer vocal notes) → Riff A → V2 → chorus → solo over verse riff → breakdown (half-time, new chug, 8 bars, builds) → final chorus ×2 → cold end stab on 1̂. Contrast comes from DRUM-MODE switches (groove↔half-time↔double/blast) more than harmony.
**8. CORNY TRAPS** — (1) Humanized/swung 16ths → metal is grid music: quantize hard, swing 50, chug vel ±6 max. (2) Kick not mirroring riff onsets → copy the gtr grid into the kick. (3) 3rds in low distorted chords → R+5 only below C3. (4) Harmonic-minor noodling everywhere = video-gamey → reserve for V/cadence bars; ride aeolian/pent otherwise. (5) Nonstop 16th wall → subtract: drop hits every 4th bar, half-time breakdown. (6) Bright IV-V-I major choruses → keep bVI-bVII-i or iv-i (unless deliberate power-metal pastiche).

## E. FOLK (fingerpicking, modal melody)
**1. CANON** — Carter Family–Wildwood Flower 1928 (Carter scratch: melody in bass strings + brush; tune itself 1860 = PD) · Elizabeth Cotten–Freight Train (alternating-thumb 2-finger picking blueprint; song ©1957 — study only) · Mississippi John Hurt–Spike Driver Blues (dead-thumb bass + syncopated treble) · Lead Belly–Midnight Special arr. (12-string boom-chick drive) · Woody Guthrie–This Land Is Your Land (strophic I-IV-V; melody from Carter-family trad stock) · Pete Seeger–Where Have All the Flowers Gone (banjo-derived calm arpeggio) · Kingston Trio–Tom Dooley (2-chord I-V trad ballad) · Joan Baez–Silver Dagger (aeolian fingerpicked drone ballad) · Dylan–Don't Think Twice (Travis-picking canon; bass-run turnarounds) · Simon & Garfunkel–The Boxer (16th Travis + add9 suspensions) · S&G–Scarborough Fair (dorian trad tune, modal harmonization; their arr. ©) · Bert Jansch–Blackwaterside (drone + ornamented modal melody) · Nick Drake–Pink Moon (altered-tuning clusters, hushed) · John Fahey–Sunflower River Blues (American-primitive melody-over-drone) · Joni Mitchell–Big Yellow Taxi (open-tuning strum, sus shimmer) · Fairport Convention–Matty Groves (dorian folk-rock band treatment) · Fleet Foxes–White Winter Hymnal (round/canon stack, pastoral triads) · Mumford & Sons–Little Lion Man (kick-stomp + 16th strum/banjo drive).
**2. HARMONY** — Core: I-IV-V strophic with 2–3 chords total (Tom Dooley = 2). Modal: dorian i-IV or i-bVII (major-IV is the dorian tell); mixolydian I-bVII (Old Joe Clark); aeolian i-bVII-bVI; double-tonic oscillation I↔bVII (Appalachian). Drone harmony: melody over static 1+5 drone, chords implied. Old-time ragtime borrow: I-VI7-II7-V7. Extensions arise from OPEN STRINGS ringing through changes (sus2/add9/sus4) — never jazz stacks. Cadences: V-I, but modal tunes bVII-I; phrases often end on 2̂ or 5̂ over V. Harmonic rhythm SLOW: 1–2 bars/chord, ballads 4. Melody-driven genre: harmony harmonizes the tune, not vice versa.
**3. COMPING** — (1) *Travis pick*: thumb quarters (0,R)(1,5)(2,R)(3,5) dur .5 vel 88 + treble pinch at 0 and hits (1.5,.5)(2.5,.5)(3.5,.5) vel 76 on chord's 3/5/O; swing 52–55; roll pinches 15ms. (2) *Carter scratch*: MELODY notes on bass strings at 0 and 2 (quarters, vel 95, G2–G3) + full-chord brush (1,.3)(3,.3) vel 80. (3) *Boom-chick*: R(0,.5,95) chord(1,.35,85) 5(2,.5,92) chord(3,.35,85); bass run in bar 4. (4) *Campfire D-DU-UDU*: (0,.5,100)(1,.5,90)(1.5,.35,75)(2.5,.35,80)(3,.5,92)(3.5,.35,75); up-strums = top 3 strings only, vel −15. (5) *Drone fingerstyle* (Jansch/Fahey): open 1-5-1 drone 8ths vel 70 + melody on top strings, sus2/4 clusters, swing 55. (6) *Mumford drive*: full-strum 8ths vel 100 → 16ths at chorus + kick quarters; banjo forward-roll 16ths (R-3-5 cycling) overlay. Signature glue: bass runs connecting chords — I→IV via (3,.5)=2̂ (3.5,.5)=3̂; V→I via 2̂ then nat-7 below.
**4. BASS** (often absent — guitar covers it; GM33 upright): (1) alternating R-5 quarters vel 85 dur .6; (2) root halves (0,2) for ballads; (3) walking quarters R-R-5-(2̂/7̧) with runs only at phrase ends; (4) tonic drone pedal that IGNORES the bVII chord above (modal tunes); (5) stomp-lock: R quarters with the kick, vel 100 (modern).
**5. MELODY GRAMMAR** — The genre's soul. Scales: major pentatonic + gapped hexatonic (Appalachian), dorian, mixolydian, aeolian; in modal tunes AVOID the leading tone (use b7). Contour: arch — rise to 5̂/6̂ mid-phrase, settle on 1̂; 4-bar phrases in AABA or ABBA; range ≤ octave+2; phrase ends = long note (≥1.5) on 1̂/2̂/5̂. Strophic literalism: SAME melody every verse; variation = arrangement layers. Ornaments: hammer-on grace (−2st, .08 overlap), 3rds double-stops on repeat verses, scotch snap. Cells: F1 (0,.5)(.5,.5)(1,.5)(1.5,.5)(2,1)(3,1); F2 snap (0,.25)(.25,.75)(1,.5)(1.5,.5)(2,2); F3 pickup (3,.5)(3.5,.5)→(0,1.5); F4 dotted lilt (0,.75)(.75,.25)(1,.75)(1.75,.25)(2,1)(3,1); F5 fiddle 8ths ×8 accents 0/2; F6 hold (0,3)(3,1); F7 call (0,.5)(.5,.5)(1,1) / response (2,.5)(2.5,.5)(3,1). **Original hooks:** H1 (dorian air): 1(0,.5) 2(.5,.5) b3(1,.5) 5(1.5,1) 4(2.5,.5) b3(3,.5) 2(3.5,.5)→1. H2 (major-pent Appalachian): pickup 5,(3.5,.5); 1(0,.75) 2(.75,.25) 3(1,.5) 5(1.5,1) 3(2.5,.5) 2(3,.5) 1(3.5,.5). H3 (mixolydian fiddle): 8ths 1-2-3-5-6-5-3-2, accents 0/2, b7 replacing 7 in the answering bar.
**6. INSTRUMENTATION** — GM26 Steel Acoustic (comp + Carter melody), GM25 Nylon (parlor), GM106 Banjo (16th forward rolls R-3-5-O), GM111 Fiddle (melody double, legato dur +10%), GM22 Harmonica (fills), mandolin fake = GM26 up an octave with tremolo (repeated 16ths dur .12). Bass GM33. Drums: NONE, or stomp kit only — K quarters + Tamb 8ths + Clap(1,3); brush fake = S vel ≤60. Tempi: ballads 70–95, mid-strum 95–120, dance/stomp 120–160.
**7. ARRANGEMENT** — Strophic ladder: V1 gtr+voice → V2 +bass → V3 +fiddle harmony (3rds) or harmonica → instrumental verse (fiddle/banjo takes the melody verbatim) → V4 drop to gtr+voice (trough) → final verse full + vocal harmony. Chorus (if any) = same chords, gang harmony. Turnaround tag: last 2 bars of each verse = bass run + V-I. Energy = LAYER COUNT, not intensity.
**8. CORNY TRAPS** — (1) Full-kit backbeat = instantly not-folk → stomp/clap/tamb or nothing. (2) Perfect-quantize even-velocity picking = MIDI campfire → swing 52–55, 10–20ms rolls, thumb louder than treble (88 vs 76). (3) Harmonizing a b7 modal melody with major V = wrongest folk sound → bVII or drone. (4) A chord every bar with jazz color → 2–3 chords total; color only from open strings. (5) Varying the melody each verse / melisma → strophic, syllabic, grace-note ornaments only. (6) Melody mid-register buried in comp → put it above (fiddle/voice) or IN the guitar bass strings (Carter), never block-harmonized note-for-note.
