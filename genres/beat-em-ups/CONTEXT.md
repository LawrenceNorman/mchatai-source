# Beat-'em-ups

Side-scrolling brawlers: walk a street in depth lanes, fight waves of enemies that come in from both
sides, a boss at the end of each stage, lives and score.

## How to build it

On the vendored versus-fighter runtime in brawler mode: read the "Brawler mode" section of
`frameworks/versus-fighter/CONTEXT.md`, model the page on `frameworks/versus-fighter/examples/iron-alley.html`,
and check it with `frameworks/versus-fighter/tools/vf-check.js`. No legacy template seed (`templateSeeding: off`).
