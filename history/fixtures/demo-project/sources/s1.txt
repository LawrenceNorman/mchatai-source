FIXTURE — fictional text for the History Writer's tests. No real person or place is described.

THE FIXTURE GAZETTE, MAY 2, 1901. Mrs. Edith Fixture was elected president of the Ladies' Library Association at its meeting on Tuesday evening, receiving eleven of the fourteen votes cast.

Mrs. Fixture came to Harbor Town from Ohio in 1890 and opened her boarding house on Water Street in 1893, at a cost of four hundred dollars. The house lodges twenty-two men from the mills.

The Association will open its reading room in the old bank building on the first of June, and Mrs. Fixture has promised one hundred books from her own shelves.
