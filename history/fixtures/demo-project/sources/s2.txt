FIXTURE — fictional text for the History Writer's tests. No real person or place is described.

Edith Fixture (1870-1934) was a boarding-house keeper and library founder in Harbor Town. Born in Ohio in 1870, she arrived in 1890 and within three years had become one of the few women in the town to own the building she did business in. Her campaign for a public reading room, waged through the Ladies' Library Association, ended in 1904 when the town council voted to fund a library in the old bank building on Water Street. She died in Harbor Town in 1934.
