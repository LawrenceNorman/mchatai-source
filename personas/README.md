# Persona packs

One folder per persona. The same pack drives the **Persona picker in iOS ChatView** and a
**hub mini-app** (`miniapps/on-device-chat/`, the on-device chat template). Design and
field notes: `mchatai_macOS/docs/PERSONA_PACKS_ARCHITECTURE.md` (Phase EX).

- `_index.json` — all records; bump `version` (YYYY.MM.DD, `.n` for a same-day change) when
  anything changes. The app ignores an index older than its built-in fallback.
- `<id>/persona.json` — the record (keep it identical to its `_index.json` entry; `_note`
  fields are the author's and may differ).
- `<id>/chunks.json` — optional knowledge: `[{id, keywords[], text, order?}]`, each ≤~110
  words. Keyword retrieval picks two or three per turn for the on-device window.
  `order` + `knowledge.gate` keep later notes out until the person has reached them
  (a book's chapters: `book-club-gatsby`).
- `<id>/tools/build_chunks.py` — when the chunks are GENERATED from a corpus (the
  Pivotal Histories dossiers for `ask-the-era`, the mchatai.com docs for `mchatai-help`),
  the generator lives beside them and the output is never edited by hand.
- `<id>/companion.md` — optional source of the pack's companion page; pictures beside it
  in `<id>/companion/`. Build with `miniapps/on-device-chat/tools/companion_page.py <id>
  <out>` and deploy the folder with the hub `deploy` verb from mChatAI+ (`plain: true`);
  the pack's `companion.url` is that address.
- `<id>/icon.png` — optional hub icon (chat uses the SF symbol in `icon`).
- `_verbs.json` — GENERATED by `verbs/build_index.py` from `verbs/ios/*.json` (Phase AV.0): the
  actions a pack with an `agent` block may call on the person's device, typed (`arguments[{name,
  type, description, noun?}]`, `inputs`/`outputs` nouns, `capability` or `steps`, `safetyClass`,
  `network`, `reason`). The same list rides inside `_index.json` as `verbs` (one fetch serves the
  chat). Edit the verb files, never these copies; the schema is `verbs/README.md`.
- `agent` in a record — `{ "verbs": [≤6 ids], "planner": "apple" | "local", "localPlannerAllowed":
  bool, "mustRun": [ids], "confirm": "plan" | "writes" | "never", "sources": [source ids] }`.
  `mustRun` ⊆ `verbs`: the steps that must have succeeded before the task counts as done (the chat
  nudges the model with the missing ones and says what was not made). `confirm` (AV.2): `plan` shows
  the intended steps and waits for OK, `writes` pauses before each step that makes something, `never`
  (default) runs. `sources` (AV.3): the source packs (`sources/_index.json`) the agent searches, first
  one by default; the person's own sites join from "My sources" on the device. The person's sentence
  runs through the verbs on their device and each call shows as a step in ChatView; "What can I ask?"
  lists `starters` and the verbs. First pack: `paper-podcaster`.
  `mode: "companion"` (Phase FL.10): the pack chats as a persona from its notes and takes one of its
  steps only when the sentence asks. It then needs `ask: {verbId: [phrases of two or more lowercase
  words]}`, and may have `afterNotes: [verbIds]`, `pointBack: {verbId: {argument, ask}}`, and beside
  the agent block `media: [{id, kind: "episode", title, path | url, seconds, chapter}]` (audio goes by `url` in the
  public downloads bucket, `gs://mchatai-downloads/personas/<pack>/media/`, never into this repo: FL.12) and
  `offers: {accept, rules: [{verb, when, phrases?, unless?, say}]}` (one offer at most per reply,
  chosen by the app), `knowledge.explain` (phrases that ask what something means: in Chat the notes
  ride only on such a sentence) and `guard: {drop: [regular expressions], empty}` (sentences an
  ordinary reply may not carry, such as advice language; also `numbersFromNotes`, `noLists`,
  `noRepeats` with `repeated` and `restate`, `shortNames: {long: short}` and `assent` with
  `{said}`/`{question}`, applied the same way in Chat and on the Hub page; FL.14 `dropWithoutNotes`, more patterns Chat
  applies only on a turn given no notes, such as a sentence quoting the handbook), and `agent.decline: [{ask, say}]` (asks
  the app answers with the pack's own line: what no step does, and advice it must never give), and before any
  decline `agent.guides: [{id, ask, also?, say, notes}]` (FL.13: answers from the pack's own text, checked against
  its sources when the pack is built; `notes` become what "that" means next). A pack with `retention: "none"` may take steps only this way, on Apple's
  planner, with `confirm: "writes"`, and only steps whose verb says what it keeps (`privateChat`).
  First pack: `money-check-in`, generated by its builder, never hand-edited. Full rules:
  `mchatai_macOS/docs/PERSONA_PACKS_ARCHITECTURE.md` §3.
- `door` (Phase FL.11) — the pack's Hub page as its front door: `{chat: {label, sub}, starterLabel, library: {item, label, sub,
  listenLabel, listenSub}, episodeSub, episodesLabel, episodesSub, quizLabel, quizSub, footer, hereLabel}`. FL.12: with more
  than one episode the page shows one Listen tile (`episodesLabel`) that opens the list, and Chat's Learn sheet shows
  `listenLabel` for the same Library item in Audio Books when the Library lists it entitled and with tracks. In an app with the pack bridge (`window.mChatAI.pack`), the page shows
  the welcome, the starters and tiles, and hands each one to the app's own code: Chat with this persona (a starter is sent as
  if tapped), Quiznect's player for a chapter's set, the episode in Podcasts, and `library.item` in AI Reader, shown only
  when the Library marks that book entitled for the account. An app without the bridge keeps the chat on the page.
- `games` (Phase FL.14) — published Hub games the pack offers: `[{id, product, url, title, label, sub, chapters, ask, say}]`.
  `product` is the Hub product id (the app's Hub player opens it over the chat, the Learn sheet or the door); `url` is its
  hosted copy, which an app without the game step opens in its browser. The door and the Learn sheet show `label`/`sub`;
  the companion opens it when a sentence says one of `ask` (verb-led phrases, no model) and offers it after an explanation
  from one of `chapters` with an `offers` rule `{verb: "openGame", when: "noteHasGame", say}`. `openGame` is the app's own
  step and never one of `agent.verbs`, so a build without it skips the rule. Words come from the game's own Hub listing.
  FL.15: `tools` (the same shape) are Hub tools such as a calculator: Learn's Calculate section, the door's 🧮 tile (feature
  `tool`), and an offer `{verb: "openTool", when: "afterCalculation"}` after a sum the app worked out. `agent.calculators:
  [{id, formula: lumpSum|monthlySaving|cardPayoff|feeDrag, ask, say, never?, missing?, notes}]` are sums the app works out
  itself before any guide. An offer rule's `line`, `button` and `send` make it a chip that sends `send` as if typed (a build
  without chips shows `say`); `door.library.ask/opening/readLabel/readSend` open the book from the chat and put a "Read
  chapter N" chip on an explanation, for an entitled account.
  A Hub page can open one chapter of a Library book over itself with `window.mChatAI.library.open({item, chapter})`
  when `window.mChatAI.pack.info()` lists `libraryChapter` in `features` and the book in `library`.
  Check everything with `python3 personas/validate_personas.py personas/_index.json`
  (tests: `python3 -m unittest discover -s personas/_tests`).
- `preinstalled: true` — in the Chat picker until the person removes it (the original six);
  every other chat pack is listed under "Get more" and joins the picker when they Get it
  (EX.7). A persona pack inside a hub mini-app they installed joins the picker by itself.

Build the hub bundle: `python3 miniapps/on-device-chat/tools/build.py <id> <out-dir>`.

Rules: `name` is the identity saved chats use — never change it, add the old one to
`aliases`. A dropped persona is `retired: true`, not deleted. No personas in Swift. Since
EX.2 ChatView picks notes from `chunks.json` per turn (the page's rules; the shared fixture
in `_tests/` pins both). Nothing financial beyond the Money Check-In demo; no health advice.
