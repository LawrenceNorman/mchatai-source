# Chapter 1: Twelve Silver

Ilse counted the coins twice on the tailboard of the cart, because the guild clerk would count them once and remember forever. Twelve silver, three of them clipped at the edge where some earlier trader had shaved the metal. She turned the clipped ones face down.

Bram watched her from the shade of the well house. "Clipped coin spends like clipped coin," he said. "Short."

"Then I'll spend it short."

The ledger hall at Karsk smelled of lamp oil and wet paper. The clerk wrote her name under the Lantern Guild seal, pressed the stamp twice, and turned the book toward her so she could read what she now owed.

[Ilse Varro — Guild Rank 1]
[Coin: 12 silver]

She read it the way she read a price board in a strange town: once for the number, once for what the number left out. Nothing on the page said who had clipped the coins. She closed the book and handed it back.

Outside, the salt carts were already moving toward the gate, wheels grinding on the flats.
