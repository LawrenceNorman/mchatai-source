# Chapter 2: The Road Out

The salt carts were already moving toward the gate, wheels grinding on the flats, when Teodor caught up with her.

"Little did they know," he said, grinning, "that the road eats apprentices." She let him have the line.

She let out a breath she didn't know she was holding. The dust was a tapestry of white and grey across the road.
